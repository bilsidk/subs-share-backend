FAQ

Is this free?
Yes. Sign up is free and you get 50 coins to start. Earn more by doing actions for other creators, or buy coin packs if you want to grow faster.
How do I earn coins?
Subscribe to channels (12 coins), like videos (5 coins), or leave comments (8 coins). Every action is verified via the YouTube API — no fake engagement.
What can I spend coins on?
Run campaigns to get real subscribers, likes, and comments on your own YouTube channel. Every engagement is from a real person, verified by YouTube.
Do subscribers stay?
Yes. Every action is API-verified and once-per-target (can't farm the same channel). If someone unsubscribes within 30 days, coins are reclaimed automatically.
